Questions, answered

What is the price of SFIVA hair oil in Pakistan?

What if I want to return it?
See our refund and returns policy — unopened bottles can be returned within 14 days of delivery.
How often should I use it?
Two to three times a week is the sweet spot. Warm a little between your palms, work it into the scalp in small circles for three or four minutes, leave it at least an hour — overnight is better — then wash out with a mild shampoo.
How long until I notice anything?
Most people say the scalp feels calmer and less itchy within the first week or two. Changes you can see in the mirror take longer, because hair grows roughly a centimetre a month. Give it a full eight-week course before you judge it, which is why the three-bottle pack is the one we recommend.
Will it make my hair greasy?
It is an oil, so it will feel rich going on. It is light enough to sleep in without ruining a pillowcase, and one wash with a mild shampoo clears it completely.
Can men use it?
Yes, and many do. The routine is identical.
Does it smell of onion?
No. We use a cold onion extract rather than pressed juice, and the kalonji and curry leaf carry the scent. It smells herbal and warm — closest to a traditional champi oil.
Is it safe for coloured or chemically treated hair?
Yes. There is nothing in the blend that strips colour. If your scalp is currently irritated from a treatment, wait until it has settled and patch-test first.
What is in it, exactly?
Seven cold-pressed botanicals and nothing else: coconut, red onion, kalonji, fenugreek, mustard, curry leaf and aloe vera. No mineral oil, no silicones, no synthetic fragrance, no parabens.
Should I patch-test?
Please do, especially if you have sensitive skin or a known allergy to any of the seven botanicals. Put a little behind the ear, leave it 24 hours, and check for a reaction before using it on the scalp.
Is onion oil or kalonji oil better for hair?
They do different jobs, which is why SFIVA uses both. Red onion extract brings sulphur compounds that support the scalp, and kalonji (black seed) is rich in the antioxidant thymoquinone and conditioning fatty acids. Blended in cold-pressed coconut oil, together with fenugreek, mustard, curry leaf and aloe, they are lighter and far more pleasant to use than either oil on its own.
Does it help with hair fall?
SFIVA is a cosmetic hair oil, not a medicine. Used two to three times a week, it nourishes the scalp and strengthens the lengths, and most customers notice less breakage and softer, glossier hair over eight weeks. If you are losing hair suddenly or in patches, please see a dermatologist.
