Before you order

Questions, answered

How do I choose my size?
Open the size guide beside the sizes — it lists chest and waist measurements for every size. Between two sizes? Choose the larger for a relaxed fit, or message us on WhatsApp and we will help you choose.
How do I care for it?
Machine wash cold inside out. Do not bleach. Line dry in shade. Do not iron the waistband.
How do I pay?
Card (Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay), or cash on delivery in Pakistan and the UAE. Delivery is free when you pay by card.
When will it arrive?
Pakistan: 2–4 working days · Pakistan nationwide. UAE: 1–2 working days · across the UAE. USA: Tracked delivery across the United States. Every order has a live status link.
Can I return it?
Unopened items can be returned within 14 days. Your order link shows every step, and our team answers on WhatsApp.
Do you sell wholesale?
Yes — shops, pharmacies, corporates and resellers buy by the carton at confidential prices. Open a partner account from the Earn with SFIVA page.
