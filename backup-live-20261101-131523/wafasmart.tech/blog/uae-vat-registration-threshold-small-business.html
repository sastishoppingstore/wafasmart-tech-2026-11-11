UAE VAT Registration Threshold for Small Businesses

UAE VAT registration thresholds explained: AED 375,000 mandatory, AED 187,500 voluntary, and what small Dubai and Abu Dhabi businesses should prepare.

Short answer: As of 2026, UAE VAT registration is mandatory when taxable supplies exceed AED 375,000 in a 12-month period. A business can register voluntarily once supplies exceed AED 187,500. Confirm the current rules with the FTA before acting.

The two thresholds

Mandatory registration: AED 375,000 of taxable supplies in the previous 12 months, or expected taxable supplies in the next 30 days. Voluntary registration: AED 187,500. These figures are the commonly cited UAE thresholds; always check the FTA for the current position.

Taxable supplies include standard-rated and zero-rated supplies. Exempt supplies are generally not counted in the same way. If your revenue is close to a threshold, get advice before you assume you are outside the system.

When the 12-month test applies

The mandatory test is usually applied by looking back at the previous 12 months or forward at the next 30 days. A seasonal business can cross the threshold in a busy month even if the annual average looks low. Keep monthly sales totals so you can see the trend before the deadline.

  • Review total taxable sales every month.
  • Separate standard-rated, zero-rated and exempt sales.
  • Watch for a single large contract that pushes you over the limit.
  • Register before the legal deadline, not after the first invoice.

Why register voluntarily?

Voluntary registration can let a small business recover input VAT on purchases and look more established to corporate customers. It also adds compliance work: VAT invoices, records, returns and payments. Register because the numbers and customers justify it, not only because a competitor is registered.

What to prepare before registering

  1. Trade licence, owner details and contact information.
  2. Sales records for the relevant 12-month period.
  3. Purchase invoices and expense records.
  4. A simple bookkeeping system that separates net, VAT and gross.
  5. A process for issuing compliant tax invoices and credit notes.
  6. A calendar for return deadlines and payments.

After registration

You will receive a Tax Registration Number (TRN). Use it on invoices and returns. Charge VAT on standard-rated supplies, keep records, and file by the due date. A calculator helps check individual lines, but your accounting system should produce the return totals.

Late registration and late filing can attract penalties. If you are unsure whether you have crossed a threshold, speak to a UAE tax adviser early. It is cheaper than fixing a missed registration later.

Frequently asked questions

What is the UAE VAT registration threshold?

Mandatory registration generally applies at AED 375,000 of taxable supplies; voluntary registration is available from AED 187,500. Confirm the current rules with the FTA.

Do zero-rated sales count toward the threshold?

Taxable supplies, including zero-rated supplies, generally count. Exempt supplies are treated differently. Check your specific mix with a tax adviser.

Can a small freelancer register voluntarily?

A business below the mandatory threshold may be able to register voluntarily if it meets the conditions. Consider input VAT recovery and compliance costs first.

What happens if I register late?

Late registration can lead to penalties. Register as soon as you know you have crossed the threshold and keep evidence of your sales records.
